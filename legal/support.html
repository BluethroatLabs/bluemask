Questions and bugs stay in the repository.

Open an issue on BluethroatLabs/bluemask. There is no separate ticket desk, chat, or account support, and no promised reply time.

What to include

  • Browser and operating system.
  • The build ID from Tests, when you have it.
  • What you expected, what happened, and the steps.
  • A synthetic image, if a picture is needed to show the problem.

Do not attach private photos, documents, seed phrases, or other secrets. A public issue is readable by anyone.

Security

Suspected privacy or security failures do not belong in a public issue. Email saxenism@bluethroatlabs.com. The repository’s security policy explains what to send. This is not a bug-bounty program.

Using the editor

Choose an image, draw over the detail, review it at 100%, and download the PNG. For a sensitive image, use the offline edition and disconnect the device before you open the file. About, in the editor, answers the privacy questions.
