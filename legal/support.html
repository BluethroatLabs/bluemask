Support for BlueMask is provided through its public repository.

General issues

Open a GitHub issue with your browser, operating system, build ID, expected behavior, actual behavior, and concise reproduction steps. Use a synthetic image and do not post sensitive material.

Security or privacy reports

Email saxenism@bluethroatlabs.com for suspected privacy or security problems. Do not place sensitive reproduction material in a public issue.

Useful evidence

If the problem concerns export, retain the exported file and its hash. If it concerns network activity, note when it occurred and whether it may have come from the hosting platform, browser, or an extension.
