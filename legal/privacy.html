BlueMask processes selected images locally in your browser. It has no application feature for uploading, tracking, or storing your images.

Images and exports

Your selected image remains in browser memory while you edit. Clearing the session releases BlueMask’s canvases and state, but does not claim forensic erasure from browser memory, swap, backups, downloads, or software outside BlueMask.

Exports use a fresh canvas, a generic filename, and do not copy the original file’s metadata. The source file on disk is unchanged.

Network and hosting

The hosting provider can receive ordinary connection metadata when it serves this site. Editing does not require a server. External links and downloads are network actions you choose.

Paranoia Mode explains how to download the standalone edition, disconnect your device, edit, export, and close BlueMask before reconnecting.

Limits

BlueMask removes the source pixels inside secure regions. It does not hide details outside those regions, make the rest of an image anonymous, or conceal a visit to the hosted site.
