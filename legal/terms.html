BlueMask is a release-candidate image-masking tool operated by Bluethroat Labs.

Use and review

You control which parts of an image are covered. Review every exported PNG at full size before sharing it, including edges, reflections, repeated details, and identifying context outside the selected regions.

Masking methods

Secure masking replaces covered pixels with an opaque patch. Cosmetic blur is an appearance effect and may leave recoverable information. Do not rely on cosmetic blur for sensitive details.

Release status

BlueMask is not presented as complete anonymity, independent security certification, or protection against information left visible elsewhere in an image. Published tests describe only their recorded fixtures, models, and conditions.

Responsible use

Use BlueMask only with images you are permitted to process and share. You remain responsible for the image you publish and for checking the downloaded result.
