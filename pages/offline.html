Use BlueMask offline

The offline edition packages the BlueMask editor into one HTML file. It can mask and export an image without contacting a processing server.

Download the offline edition ↓

Offline workflow

  1. Download the offline edition.
  2. Verify its SHA-256 hash against the published build manifest.
  3. Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data and unplug Ethernet.
  4. Open the downloaded HTML file.
  5. Choose the image, apply secure masks, and review every covered region at 100%.
  6. Export and inspect the flattened PNG.
  7. Close BlueMask before reconnecting the device.

Your device controls its network connection. BlueMask cannot disconnect it for you.

Opening BlueMask.html directly from disk disables the offline-edition, source, manifest, and evidence downloads. Links to Bluethroat Labs and the research references remain in the file. They need a network connection, and masking does not use them. Privacy, Terms, and Support are separate pages, so those relative links are not available inside the single downloaded file.

What offline use proves

Successfully using the editor while disconnected demonstrates that masking and export do not require a server.

It does not prove that the BlueMask file, browser, extensions, operating system, or device is trustworthy. Review the published source and hashes when the sensitivity of the image warrants it.

The retained offline check opens this editor with browser network emulation disabled. It is not a physical radio-off test on a phone.

Accepted images: PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 35 MB (35 × 1024 × 1024 bytes), 24 megapixels, and 12,000 pixels on the longer side.

Keyboard editing: add a region, change its coordinates, undo, redo, and export from the controls. Dragging to draw, move, or resize uses a pointer.

Browser evidence: headless Chrome only. No retained Firefox or Safari desktop result. Phone results are emulated, not physical devices.
